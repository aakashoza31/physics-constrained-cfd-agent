Simulate a 2D forward-facing step in the validated supersonic channel family
with inlet Mach number 3.0, step height 0.20 channel heights, and step location
x = 0.6.

Use the registered inviscid compressible Euler forward-step family and its
validated OpenFOAM shockFluid numerical recipe.

Start from a deliberately coarse but valid mesh: use 120 cells across the
channel length and 40 across the channel height.

Assess whether the forward-step result has adequate numerical resolution. Do
not treat a healthy solution on a single grid as evidence that the answer is
resolved. If the evidence shows the result still depends on the grid, refine
the mesh through the registered bounded refinement action and reassess.

Report the compression and shock structure on each grid and how it changes
between grids.
